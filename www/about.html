About the club

What Aghimuan stands for.

A student-run ICT organization at PCU-D, built around three core ideals: making, sharing, and innovating responsibly.

⚡ Vision

To be a leading community that empowers students to innovate, collaborate, and excel in the field of information and communications technology, driving technological progress and fostering inclusive growth for all.

🎯 Mission

  1. To build an open-source community that welcomes students from diverse backgrounds to learn, share, and grow together in the ICT field.
  2. To provide opportunities for members to develop new technological skills through hands-on projects, workshops, and collaborative activities.
  3. To encourage ethical and responsible use of technology and foster critical thinking and innovation that positively impacts society.
  4. To create a platform where students can connect, exchange ideas, and work on real-world ICT solutions that contribute to their personal and professional development.

👥 Membership

Membership in the AGHIMUAN Club is open to all students, not limited to those in Senior High School, in alignment with the club's mission to promote inclusivity and collaboration.

Whether you are getting started in code or tinkering with robotics hardware, there is space to learn and build.

🏫 At PCU-D

Aghimuan operates as the ICT student organization of Philippine Christian University - Dasmariñas, giving members a shared space to apply what they learn in class to robotics builds, programming projects, and system servicing work alongside peers across year levels.
