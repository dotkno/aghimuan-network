Escape together. Level 1 is training. After that, each level has key rooms, gem vaults, and one sealed exit vault. Collect every gem, press the EXIT KEY (gold ring), then stand both players on the green exit pads at the same time.

Cyan buttons are Player 1 only and open cyan doors. Orange buttons are Player 2 only. A key sitting in goo can only be reached by the matching player. Split up and open doors for each other. The white arrow points to your next objective.

Goo kills: cyan goo kills Player 2, orange goo kills Player 1, red goo kills everyone. Your own color is safe.

Zones in order: TRAINING, NEON TUNNELS, GOO CAVERNS, THE VAULT. Later zones add locked gems (white ring, owner only) and teleport pads (purple rings).
